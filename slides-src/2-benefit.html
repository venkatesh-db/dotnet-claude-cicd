THE BENEFIT, IN PLAIN TERMS

Nothing waits on a person to start

WITHOUT — a person has to do it first

WITH CLAUDE — Claude does it first

Someone opens a PR

A teammate reviews it

whenever they're free — hours to days

Claude reviews it first

automatically — about a minute

A build breaks

Someone digs through logs

once they notice — 20-30+ minutes

Claude explains why

opens an issue with the root cause

It needs a fix

Someone writes the fix

whenever it fits into their day

A draft fix is waiting

a real PR, ready for someone to check

A person still decides what merges — Claude just makes sure nothing waits on a person to start.
